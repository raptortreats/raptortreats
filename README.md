## About

I work at the overlap of **machine learning**, **quantum computing**, and **molecular science** — mostly on models that help design or evaluate small molecules, plus LLM systems that can actually cite what they retrieve.

- 🏢 **Applied AI Scientist / AI Engineer** at [PharmCADD](https://pharmcadd.com)

## What I work on

| Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Quantum ML** | Quantum Circuit Born Machines, quantum kernels (`QSVC`), hybrid quantum-classical training loops on CPU simulators (Qiskit, PennyLane) |
| **Drug Discovery AI** | Molecular property prediction, goal-directed analog generation, cheminformatics with RDKit |
| **Generative / Predictive models** | Discrete generative models vs classical chemical LMs; QSAR-style solubility classification and regression |
| **Applied LLM systems** | RAG over scientific / medical literature, FastAPI services, agent-style workflows |

Public repos here are **portfolio-scale and honest**: simulator qubits, documented subsets, and baselines — not quantum-advantage claims or production drug pipelines.
